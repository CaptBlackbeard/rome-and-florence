# Rome & Florence in April

A family trip plan for Rome and Florence with a small child.

**Live site:** https://captblackbeard.github.io/rome-and-florence/
**Map:** https://captblackbeard.github.io/rome-and-florence/map/

## What's on the site

- Day-by-day itinerary for both cities
- Hotels ranked by walkability
- Points of interest, restaurants and coffee, with Tripadvisor ratings
- Train stations, plus advice on booking train tickets in advance
- Interactive map with walking-distance circles

## Notes

- The site is static HTML served by GitHub Pages; there is no build step in this repo.
- Pages are marked `noindex`, so search engines are asked not to list them.
- Photo credits and sources are listed at the bottom of the page.
- Music: *Passo mezzo e mascherada* (Respighi, *Ancient Airs and Dances*), played by the United States Marine Band. Public domain.
